🏛️ About the College

Bhandary Foundation (R) was established in 2001 with a vision to impart societal development in the fields of education, health, sports and culture. It established Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management in 2007.

The campus spreads over 35 acres on the bank of the Netravati River with a combined workforce and student strength of nearly 4,000 and approximately 6.00 lakh sq-ft of built-up area, including hostels. The college offers B.E, M.Tech & MBA along with VTU-recognised research centres for M.Sc (Eng) and Ph.D, and is recognised by AICTE, UGC and MoE.

NAAC ‘A’ Grade NBA Accredited (CS, IS, EC, MECH) IE(I) DSIR-SIRO recognised Top 25 Most Innovative Private Institution (NIRF) 4-star MHRD-IIC ranking

🎯 Vision

To be a premier institution in Technology and Management by fostering excellence in education, innovation, incubation and values to inspire and empower the young minds.

🚀 Mission

  • Create an academic ambience to impart holistic education focusing on individual growth, integrity, ethical values and social responsibility.
  • Develop skill-based learning through industry-institution interaction to enhance competency and promote entrepreneurship.
  • Foster innovation and creativity through a competitive environment with state-of-the-art infrastructure.

📚 Programs & Learning

  • 9 Undergraduate (UG) degree programmes and a Post Graduate (PG) degree programme, with around 3,000 students from culturally diversified backgrounds.
  • B.E, M.Tech & MBA, plus VTU research centres for M.Sc (Eng) and Ph.D.
  • Project-Based Learning (PBL) for community-centric engineering solutions.
  • Work spaces for AIML, Embedded System Design, Industry 4.0, IoT, 3D Printing and Robotics.
  • A 250-seater skill lab, a 100-seater GIG workspace and blue-chip company partnerships.

💡 Innovation & Incubation

  • Dedicated innovation and acceleration facility supported by K-Tech Innovation, Govt. of Karnataka.
  • Host Institute (HI) for funding under Technical Business Incubation (TBI) by MSME, Govt. of India.
  • Part of the Karnataka Acceleration Network — scaling 30 startups every year.
  • A 100-seater incubation facility supported by ACIC, NITI Aayog.
  • Centre of Excellence in Future Technology supported by VGST, Govt. of Karnataka.
  • 22 start-up companies established by students on campus; 1.50 lakh sq-ft dedicated to industries and start-ups.
